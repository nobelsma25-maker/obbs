5 FACTS ABOUT MINECRAFT

The Creeper was Created by a Coding Error

While coding a 3D pig model for Minecraft, Markus "Notch" Persson accidentally swapped its height and length dimensions. The resulting tall, thin creature was given a green texture and developed into the iconic explosive mob known as the Creeper.

Ghast Sounds Come from a Disturbed Sleeping Cat

The iconic, haunting sounds of the Ghast were created by composer C418 by pitching, stretching, and editing audio recordings of his cat being suddenly awakened from sleep.

A Single Minecraft World is Larger than Planet Earth

A Minecraft world spans up to 60 million blocks in diameter, covering roughly 3.6 billion square kilometers—making its virtual surface area about eight times larger than Earth.

Endermen Speak English Backwards and Pitch-Shifted

The alien noises and whispers emitted by Endermen are actually reversed, pitch-lowered, and distorted recordings of human voices speaking English phrases like "hello" and "what's up?"

Minecraft Was Created in Just Six Days

The first playable build of Minecraft, originally called Cave Game, was created by Markus Persson in just one week in May 2009 and published on an online gaming forum for early player feedback.
